# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected

- Welcoming, patient responses — especially to first-time contributors.
- Disagreement argued on technical merit, with reasons.
- Accepting a maintainer decision gracefully once it is made, and revisiting it
  with new evidence rather than repetition.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or review.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits and any other project space.

Report a problem to the maintainer through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/ErikBurdett/omarchy-tokenstats/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that breach
this document, and may block repeat offenders.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately.
